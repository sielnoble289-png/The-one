Saved strategy mini bot

Tick the strategies you saved (they can be on different markets), set a starting balance, and the mini bot runs all of them together on real Deriv ticks, sharing one balance. Each time the balance reaches the next multiple of your starting balance (for example $10 → $20 → $40) every strategy’s lot size is raised by the same step. The order you tick them in is their priority. Each strategy keeps its own daily profit and loss limits; edit them below or set either to 0 to turn it off.

How it works: with a reserve, only the rest of your balance (the trading money, for example $10 of a $20 account) sets the lot size: the lot doubles when the trading money doubles, and the reserve stays out of that sum. The reserve still counts as part of your balance, so it absorbs losses and stops the account being blown until everything is gone. Every strategy keeps its own saved market, timeframe, chart type, indicators, stop loss, take profit, exit rule and direction; its saved lot size is the lot at your starting balance and is multiplied as the balance grows (kept inside Deriv’s own lot limits and steps). A new lot only applies to the next trade, never to one already open. A stop loss or take profit typed here replaces the saved one on every ticked strategy (in $ it is worked out at each strategy’s saved lot, which is the lot at your starting balance); leave it at 0 to keep each strategy’s own. At most the number of trades you set can be open at the same time: if more signals arrive together, the first ones by priority open and the rest are dropped, and a dropped signal is never taken late. MT5 mode profit = price move × lot. Multipliers mode estimates profit from percentage price movement × stake × multiplier, with loss capped at stake. Choose whether the stop loss and take profit grow with the lot or stay the same $, and whether they fill exactly at the level or at the first tick past it. Without a cap on the lot multiplier the lot keeps doubling for as long as the balance does, which can make the multiples look unrealistic, so set a cap to see a more cautious result. Each selected strategy has its own daily target and daily loss cap, loaded from its saved settings and editable in the strategy list. Reaching either limit closes that strategy's open trade and pauses that strategy until the next UTC day. A max trade time override can replace all saved trade times. Margin, spread and swaps are not modelled, and the account counts as blown when the balance plus the floating profit or loss of all the trades open at that moment, together, reaches zero (checked on every tick), or when a closed trade takes the balance to zero.
